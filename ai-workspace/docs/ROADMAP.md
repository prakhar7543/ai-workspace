# AI Workspace - Roadmap

Version: 1.0
Status: Active

---

# Vision

Build AI Workspace into a modern AI-powered productivity platform that showcases professional frontend engineering practices while providing a strong foundation for future backend, authentication, and AI integrations.

The roadmap is divided into milestones and sprints. Each sprint delivers a complete, testable increment of the application.

---

# Roadmap Rules

- Only one sprint may be active at a time.
- Finish the current sprint before starting the next.
- Every sprint must produce a working application.
- Documentation must be updated after each completed sprint.
- Architecture changes require updating ARCHITECTURE.md.
- Completed work must be reflected in PROJECT_STATUS.md.
